# Lungo — Free Offline Installer

![Category](https://img.shields.io/badge/Caffeination_Tool-blue?style=flat-square)

## About

Lungo is a free macOS menu bar utility that prevents your Mac from going to sleep or activating the screen saver. Inspired by the espresso drink, Lungo keeps your Mac awake during downloads, presentations, or any task where you don't want the display to sleep. Simple toggle from the menu bar with configurable duration makes it the most minimal solution for keeping your Mac awake.

## Common Problems & Limitations

- **macOS Only** — macOS-specific system utility
- **Energy Impact** — Preventing sleep increases energy consumption on laptops
- **Minimal Features** — Single-purpose tool; no scheduling or complex configuration
- **System Preference Alternative** — macOS energy settings can achieve the same result manually
